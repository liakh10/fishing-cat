Fishing Cat, the log

He catches them and he puts them back One jetty, one line, and a rule he has never broken

01 · OverviewWhat is Fishing Cat

Everybody else on this water is trying to land something and keep it. He sits on a crate at the end of a wooden jetty, casts once, waits, brings something up, writes it in the log, and drops it back in.

The whole site is that loop, printed in four inks. Nothing to press, nothing to scroll. Watch it long enough and you will see the same six things come out of the water, and every one of them goes back.

Format
One fixed scene, one loop, about eight seconds a cast
Style
Screen print in four inks: teal, coral, mustard, cream
Kept
Nothing, not once
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · The lineIt is really hanging there

The fishing line is not a drawn curve. It is twenty two points with velocity and gravity, pulled back into shape eight times a frame until every segment is the right length again. That is why it sags when he waits and snaps straight when he lifts.

Points
Twenty two, first pinned to the rod tip, last to the hook
Water
Drawn every frame, two sine waves crossing, no image
The float
Rides the surface, dips when something takes it

03 · The logCaught and released

the log ├── a doge back wanted to know where the moon was ├── a pepe back green already, no notes ├── an old boot back third time this week ├── a laptop back still logged in, put it straight back ├── a bag back somebody was holding this └── one good fish back released, obviously
